# ExamSaathi AI – Solution Pitch

**Project:** ExamSaathi AI  
**Developer:** Solo Project  
**GitHub:** https://github.com/yashikk507-cmyk/EXAM-SATHI-AI

### Problem
Students often struggle to understand difficult academic topics, find answers in lengthy study materials, and access personalized learning support. Traditional study methods may not provide interactive explanations or convenient voice-based assistance.

### Solution
ExamSaathi AI is an AI-powered learning assistant designed to make studying simpler and more accessible. It allows students to ask academic questions through text or voice, receive easy-to-understand explanations, and ask questions directly from uploaded PDF study materials.

### Key Features
- **AI Tutor:** Answers academic questions in simple language.
- **Voice Interaction:** Converts spoken questions into text and generates spoken answers.
- **PDF-Based Learning:** Allows students to upload notes and ask questions based on their content.
- **Multilingual Support:** Supports English and Hindi interaction.

### Technology Used
- **Gnani AI Vachana – Prisma v3.0:** Speech-to-text.
- **Gnani AI Timbre v2.5:** Text-to-speech.
- Python and FastAPI for backend development.
- HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the frontend.
- Ollama with Qwen 2.5 3B for answer generation.
- ChromaDB and Nomic embeddings for PDF-based retrieval.

### Impact
ExamSaathi AI aims to help students understand academic concepts, interact naturally with study material, and learn more effectively through text, voice, and document-based assistance.
